# TORCLIX FIT monetization

## Free
Basic onboarding, limited workouts, basic progress and streak.

## PRO
Adaptive NEXT MOVE, Fitness Twin, AI Decision Center, recovery intelligence, nutrition intelligence, forecasts and advanced analytics.

## Paid principle
The user pays for better decisions and faster progress, not for a larger exercise library.

## Payment
YooKassa is the planned payment provider for the Russian launch. Stripe is intentionally not part of the current plan.
