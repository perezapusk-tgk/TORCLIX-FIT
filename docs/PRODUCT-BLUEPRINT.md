# TORCLIX FIT — Product Blueprint v1

## Core promise
**Don't guess your next step. TORCLIX FIT calculates it.**

## Core loop
Data → analysis → NEXT MOVE → action → result → adaptation.

## Modules
1. Today
2. Training
3. Fitness Twin
4. Evolve
5. AI Decision Center
6. Nutrition
7. Recovery
8. Missions
9. Squads / Duels
10. Profile / settings

## Premium
- Adaptive NEXT MOVE
- Fitness Twin
- advanced AI decisions
- nutrition and recovery intelligence
- forecasting / What If
- deep progress analytics
- advanced missions

## Design
Obsidian + Graphite + Titanium Gold + Ice Blue. Cinematic imagery, restrained glass, strong typography, large numbers, high contrast, minimal visual noise.

## Launch funnel
Short video → Telegram bot → Mini App → onboarding → first NEXT MOVE → first completed workout → PRO trial → subscription → referral.

## Content channels
TikTok, YouTube Shorts, VK Clips, Instagram Reels, Telegram, YouTube long-form.

## Content engine
One core idea becomes a long video, several short videos, Telegram post, stories, carousel, and result card.

## Roadmap
### Phase 1
Mini App foundation, onboarding, profile, goals, training log, NEXT MOVE, Telegram bot, analytics.

### Phase 2
Fitness Twin, nutrition, recovery, AI Decision Center, missions, subscriptions.

### Phase 3
Squads, duels, referrals, coach mode.

### Phase 4
Wearables, health integrations, marketplace.
