# TORCLIX FIT — Product Blueprint v1

## Core promise
Stop guessing what to do today. TORCLIX FIT calculates your next best move.

## Core loop
Data → interpretation → NEXT MOVE → action → result → adaptation.

## Main areas
Today — readiness, explanation, NEXT MOVE and daily target.
Train — adaptive workouts, sets, load, rest timer and technique.
Body — Fitness Twin, measurements and photos.
Evolve — strength, body, consistency and goal progress.
AI — explainable decisions: what changed, why, what to do.
Nutrition — goal-oriented food guidance.
Recovery — sleep, load and recovery signals.
Missions — 30/60/90-day goals and accountability.

## Premium
PRO unlocks adaptive NEXT MOVE, deeper Fitness Twin, advanced AI decisions, recovery, nutrition intelligence, forecasting and deeper analytics.

## Rules
The interface answers the next-action question before showing analytics.
AI recommendations are explainable.
Data and deterministic rules come before generative reasoning.
No medical diagnosis or treatment claims.
Privacy-sensitive metrics are private by default.
